01 — About

Engineering systems that work in production

I'm a mechatronics engineer with a national diploma in embedded systems from ESPRIMs, Monastir, built on a bachelor in physics. My work sits where robotics, vision and embedded software meet: bringing KUKA, ABB and Universal Robots cells online, wiring Modbus/TCP between PLCs and vision applications, and writing the Python and C++ that keep it all running.

Beyond the code, I've spent my career in team leadership, training and public relations. That means short distances between disciplines — mechanical, controls, IT and operations — and a bias toward documentation and clear handovers.

I also teach. ROS courses for engineering students at Polytech Monastir, robotics coaching through Top-Niveau, and PFE supervision on industrial embedded topics (IoT energy optimisation, machine vision). I like problems where the honest answer is "measure it first", not "add a model".
