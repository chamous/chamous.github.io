01 — About

Keeping production lines running and improving

I lead industrial automation and maintenance in automotive manufacturing, with a mechatronics engineering background from ESPRIMs, Monastir built on a bachelor in physics. My work sits where robotics, vision and production engineering meet: keeping ABB, KUKA and Universal Robots cells running, wiring Modbus between PLCs and vision applications, and shipping the Python and C++ behind them.

The maintenance half of the job matters just as much. Predictive maintenance strategy, root cause analysis and OEE improvement are where downtime is actually won back — and I've seen the gain from tying them together with IoT and real-time monitoring on Raspberry Pi and Linux.

Beyond the technical work, I manage teams of technicians and engineers, control CAPEX and budgets, coordinate with suppliers, and write the technical specifications (cahiers des charges) that new machines and automated cells are built from. I also train and mentor: ROS courses for engineering students at Polytech Monastir, robotics coaching through TopNiveau, and PFE supervision on industrial embedded topics.
